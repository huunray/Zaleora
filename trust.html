Zaleora connects people to skills, knowledge, opportunities, collaboration and markets. That connection depends on trust.

Privacy, safety, authenticity, intellectual property, responsible technology and respect for cultural knowledge are therefore important parts of the Zaleora experience.

1.Data Protection and Privacy

Zaleora is committed to responsible handling of personal information and transparent communication about how information is collected, used, shared and protected. Read the full Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice.

2.Account and Identity Integrity

  • Provide accurate information.
  • Use only work and information you have the right to present.
  • Protect account credentials.
  • Complete verification honestly where applicable.
  • Do not impersonate another person or organisation.

3.Verification and Trust

Where available, verification provides additional information that may help users understand who they are interacting with. Verification is one element of trust and does not guarantee quality, competence, character or future performance.

4.Reviews and Reputation

Trust is strengthened by genuine experiences. Reviews should reflect real work, services, orders or engagements where the relevant feature supports them.

Fabricated or manipulated Reviews undermine the integrity of the platform and are not permitted.

5.Intellectual Property

Creative work has value. Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights or permissions for content they upload, showcase, teach, sell or share.

Zaleora aims to provide appropriate channels for reporting suspected infringement.

6.Heritage and Cultural Responsibility

Cultural knowledge can carry history, identity, meaning and community connection. Zaleora aims to present Heritage with context and respect.

The fact that a cultural practice or creative expression is visible online does not automatically make it free from cultural, intellectual-property or community considerations.

7.Safe and Respectful Community

Zaleora is intended to be a place for meaningful creative connection. Threats, harassment, hate, scams, impersonation, malicious activity, privacy violations and other harmful conduct are not acceptable.

8.Marketplace and Transactions

Where Marketplace services are available, Zaleora aims to support clear information around listings, pricing, transaction status and applicable terms. Sellers remain responsible for their offers and buyers should review transaction information before purchasing.

9.Opportunities and Fair Participation

Zaleora aims to make opportunity information understandable and discoverable. Eligibility and selection remain with the relevant opportunity provider unless otherwise stated.

10.Responsible Use of AI

AI can support discovery, recommendations, matching, learning and other Zaleora experiences. AI can also make mistakes. Important decisions should therefore be made with appropriate human judgement and, where necessary, verification of information.

11.Reporting and Safety

Users can report content or conduct that violates Zaleora policies, infringes rights or raises safety concerns through the available reporting and support channels.

12.Transparency

Zaleora aims to communicate clearly about important platform processes, including privacy, transactions, verification, Reviews, content standards and reporting.

13.Trust Principles

  1. Respect people.
  2. Respect creative work.
  3. Respect knowledge and cultural context.
  4. Value genuine work and genuine experiences.
  5. Be clear about important rules and processes.
  6. Use technology responsibly.
  7. Keep improving.
