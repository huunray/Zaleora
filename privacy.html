1.Introduction

Zaleora respects the privacy of people who use its website, applications and services. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data may be collected, used, stored, shared and protected when people interact with Zaleora.

It applies to information collected through the public website and, where applicable, Zaleora’s web and mobile applications and services.

2.About Zaleora

Zaleora is a creative technology platform connecting skills, knowledge, people, opportunities and markets. Depending on the service available, this may include Creative Identity, Academy, Heritage, Community, Collaborations, Opportunities, Marketplace, Portfolio, Reviews, messaging, payments and related services.

4.Personal Data We May Collect

  • Identity and profile information, such as name, username, profile photograph, biography and location information provided by the user.
  • Contact information such as email address and telephone number.
  • Account credentials, preferences and settings.
  • Creative and professional information, including skills, experience, Portfolio, qualifications, certifications, services, products, interests and availability.
  • Learning information, including courses, progress, assessments and certificates.
  • Community and collaboration information, including posts, messages, briefs, applications, project information and participation.
  • Marketplace and transaction information, including listings, orders, invoices, transaction status and fulfilment information.
  • Verification and trust information where verification is offered.
  • Technical and usage information, including device, browser, IP address, operating system, pages viewed, timestamps and interaction information.
  • Cookie and similar technology information.
  • Information provided when contacting support, submitting feedback, making a report or communicating with Zaleora.

5.Information Received From Other Sources

Where lawful and appropriate, Zaleora may receive information from service providers, payment providers, verification providers, authentication services, logistics or fulfilment partners, organisations working with Zaleora and other lawful sources.

6.How We Use Personal Data

  • To create and manage accounts and Creative Identities.
  • To provide and operate Zaleora services.
  • To support learning, community participation, collaborations, opportunities, marketplace activity, messaging and related features.
  • To process payments, orders, refunds, payouts and transaction records.
  • To support verification, trust, security and platform integrity.
  • To communicate about accounts, services, support, updates and important notices.
  • To understand how the platform is used and improve its performance and experience.
  • To detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents and other unlawful activity.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, accounting and record-keeping requirements.
  • To protect the rights, safety and property of Zaleora, users and others.
  • To carry out other purposes communicated at the time information is collected or otherwise permitted by law.

7.Lawful Bases for Processing

Where applicable law requires a lawful basis, Zaleora may rely on consent, performance of a contract or steps taken at a user’s request before entering a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of vital interests, or legitimate interests where those interests are not overridden by applicable rights.

8.Public Profiles and User Content

Users may choose to make parts of their Creative Identity, Portfolio, products, services, Reviews or Community contributions publicly visible. The information displayed will depend on the feature and relevant privacy settings.

Users should consider carefully what they publish and should not disclose another person’s personal information, confidential information or restricted material without the necessary authority.

9.AI and Automated Processing

Zaleora may use artificial intelligence and automated systems to support discovery, recommendations, matching, search, learning support, content organisation, analytics and other platform experiences.

AI-generated or automated outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate. Where applicable, Zaleora will provide information and safeguards required by law for material automated processing.

10.Heritage and Cultural Knowledge

Zaleora may present information relating to cultural practices, creative traditions, techniques, materials, stories and knowledge. Such information may carry cultural, community, intellectual-property or other rights and considerations.

Zaleora aims to handle Heritage content with context and respect, including appropriate attribution, access considerations, usage guidance and community perspectives where relevant.

11.Sharing Personal Data

Zaleora may share personal data with:

  • Technology and infrastructure providers.
  • Payment and financial service providers.
  • Logistics, fulfilment and transaction-support providers where applicable.
  • Authentication, verification, security, analytics and communication providers.
  • Professional advisers and service providers.
  • Regulators, law-enforcement agencies, courts or other authorities where required or permitted by law.
  • Other users or members of the public where a user has intentionally made information public.
  • Relevant parties involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring or transfer of business, subject to applicable law.

12.International Data Transfers

Some Zaleora service providers or infrastructure may process personal data outside Nigeria or outside the country where a user is located. Where required, Zaleora will use appropriate legal mechanisms and safeguards for such transfers.

13.Data Retention

Zaleora retains personal data for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, the provision of services, security, dispute resolution, legal and regulatory obligations and other lawful purposes. When information is no longer required, it may be deleted, anonymised or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable law.

14.Security

Zaleora uses appropriate technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

15.Data Protection Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may:

  • Request access to personal data held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where applicable.
  • Request restriction of processing where applicable.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request information about relevant automated processing and safeguards.
  • Make a complaint to Zaleora and, where appropriate, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or another competent authority.

16.How to Exercise Your Rights

Requests may be sent to [insert privacy email]. Zaleora may need to verify a requester’s identity before responding to certain requests. Requests will be handled within the period required by applicable law.

17.Children and Young Users

Zaleora will apply appropriate safeguards where services involve children or young people. Users must meet the age and legal requirements applicable to the service they are using.

18.Personal Data Breaches

Where Zaleora becomes aware of a personal data breach, it will assess and respond in accordance with applicable law, including any required notifications to affected individuals, regulators or other parties.

19.Third-Party Links and Services

Zaleora may contain links to or integrations with third-party services. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Zaleora is not responsible for third-party privacy practices except where applicable law provides otherwise.

20.Changes to This Policy

Zaleora may update this Privacy Policy as its services, technology and legal obligations develop. Where required, material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels.

This Policy should be read together with our Cookie Notice and Terms & Conditions.
