1.What Are Cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies are small files or technologies used by websites and applications to remember information, support functionality, understand usage and improve user experience.

2.How Zaleora May Use Cookies

  • To provide essential website and account functionality.
  • To maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • To remember preferences and settings.
  • To understand website performance and usage.
  • To improve navigation and user experience.
  • To measure communications or campaigns where permitted.

3.Types of Cookies

Essential Cookies

Used for core functions, security and services requested by the user.

Preference Cookies

Used to remember choices such as language, display or other preferences.

Analytics Cookies

Used to understand how visitors interact with the website and improve performance.

Marketing or Advertising Cookies

Where used and legally permitted, these may support campaign measurement or personalised marketing.

4.Cookie Choices

Where required by law, Zaleora will provide a mechanism for users to accept, reject or manage non-essential cookies. Browser settings may also be used to control cookies, although disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

5.Third-Party Technologies

Zaleora may use third-party technologies for analytics, security, authentication, payments, video, communications or other services. This Notice reflects only the technologies actually deployed on the live website and applications, and will be updated as that changes.

6.Cookie and Tracker Inventory

The Zaleora website does not currently set cookies, and does not use analytics, advertising pixels, SDKs or similar tracking technologies.

To deliver the website, it relies on the following third-party services, which receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser details when you visit or submit a form:

  • Vercel: website hosting and delivery.
  • Google Fonts: delivery of the typefaces used on the website.
  • Google Apps Script and Google Sheets: receiving and storing waitlist sign-ups (name and email address).

If cookies, pixels, SDKs, tags or similar technologies are introduced, this inventory will be updated to list each one’s provider, purpose, category and retention period.

7.Relationship With the Privacy Policy

Cookies and similar technologies may involve personal data. This Cookie Notice should therefore be read together with Zaleora’s Privacy Policy.

8.Updates and Contact

Zaleora may update this Notice as technology and legal requirements change.
